# Triple-Hay Concept LLC
## Executive Briefing Standard
### RoyalOS Leadership Library v1.0

Version: 1.0

Owner: Executive Office

Status: Approved

Classification: Executive Intelligence

---

# PURPOSE

The Executive Briefing is the official communication format used by RoyalOS to report important information to the CEO.

Every AI employee must use this format when completing missions, reporting progress, presenting research, recommending actions, or requesting executive approval.

Executive Briefings reduce confusion.

Executive Briefings improve decision-making.

Executive Briefings become permanent Company Intelligence.

---

# OBJECTIVE

Every Executive Briefing should answer one question:

"What does the CEO need to know in order to make the best decision?"

The briefing should remove unnecessary information while preserving everything important.

---

# WHEN TO USE AN EXECUTIVE BRIEFING

Executive Briefings are required for:

Completed Missions

Major Research

Business Recommendations

Marketing Campaigns

Product Launches

Strategic Decisions

Technology Projects

Security Issues

Operational Changes

Performance Reviews

Risk Assessments

Investment Opportunities

Customer Issues requiring Executive Review

Any decision requiring CEO approval

---

# EXECUTIVE BRIEFING FORMAT

Every Executive Briefing must contain the following sections.

---

MISSION

Provide a one-sentence description of the assignment.

Example:

Develop a launch strategy for the new ChoiceRoyals AI course.

---

OBJECTIVE

Explain what the mission was intended to accomplish.

Focus on business outcomes rather than tasks.

---

BUSINESS CONTEXT

Why does this mission matter?

How does it support:

Company Mission

Company Vision

Business Strategy

Current Priorities

Long-Term Growth

---

EMPLOYEES INVOLVED

Identify every participating AI employee.

Example

Executive Office

Marketing

Research

Analytics

Automation

---

WORK COMPLETED

Summarize completed work.

Keep it organized.

Use clear bullet points.

Focus on completed deliverables.

---

KEY FINDINGS

Identify important discoveries.

Research

Customer Insights

Analytics

Technology

Market Intelligence

Opportunities

Problems

Lessons Learned

---

RECOMMENDATIONS

Provide professional recommendations.

Each recommendation should include reasoning.

Recommendations should always be actionable.

---

RISKS

Identify:

Business Risks

Technical Risks

Operational Risks

Financial Risks

Brand Risks

Customer Risks

Every risk should include a proposed mitigation strategy.

---

NEXT ACTIONS

List the immediate next steps.

Identify ownership.

Recommend execution order.

Estimate priority.

---

CEO APPROVAL

Clearly identify whether approval is required.

Options:

Approval Required

Approval Not Required

Information Only

Urgent Review Required

---

MISSION STATUS

One of the following:

Completed

In Progress

Waiting for Approval

Blocked

On Hold

Cancelled

Archived

---

PRIORITY

Every briefing receives one priority level.

Critical

High

Normal

Low

Future

---

KNOWLEDGE CAPTURE

Every Executive Briefing should identify:

New Knowledge

Updated Documentation

New SOPs

Lessons Learned

Future Improvements

This information becomes Company Intelligence.

---

# EXECUTIVE WRITING STANDARD

Executive Briefings should be:

Professional

Objective

Concise

Strategic

Well Organized

Evidence Based

Actionable

Never emotional.

Never disorganized.

Never speculative without clearly identifying assumptions.

---

# EXECUTIVE DECISION SUPPORT

RoyalOS should never simply present information.

Every Executive Briefing should help the CEO answer:

What happened?

Why does it matter?

What are my options?

What do you recommend?

What happens next?

---

# BRIEFING QUALITY CHECK

Before submitting every Executive Briefing ask:

Is it complete?

Is it accurate?

Is it organized?

Is it useful?

Does it support decision-making?

Can the CEO understand it within two minutes?

Does it strengthen Company Intelligence?

If not, improve it before submission.

---

# COMPANY INTELLIGENCE RULE

Every approved Executive Briefing becomes permanent Company Intelligence.

RoyalOS should preserve:

Recommendations

Research

Lessons Learned

Successful Strategies

Failures

Metrics

Executive Decisions

Future employees should benefit from today's work.

Increase clarity.
