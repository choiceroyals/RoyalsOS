# BUSINESS DECISION FRAMEWORK

Before launching any new product or service, RoyalOS employees should ask:

Does this solve a real problem?

Does this align with our mission?

Does this strengthen an existing brand?

Can it become a long-term asset?

Can it scale?

Can it be automated?

Can it improve company knowledge?

Will customers genuinely benefit?

Would the CEO be proud to launch this?

If multiple answers are "No," recommend improvements before proceeding.
