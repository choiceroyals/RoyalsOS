# RoyalOS Knowledge Engine
## Knowledge Retrieval & Intelligence Framework

Version: 1.0

Owner: Executive Office

Status: Active

Classification: Core System Intelligence

---

# PURPOSE

The Knowledge Engine is responsible for ensuring every RoyalOS employee has access to the right knowledge at the right time.

Employees should never answer from memory alone.

Employees should retrieve relevant company knowledge before reasoning.

The objective is consistent, accurate and continuously improving executive intelligence.
